ELEPHANTFIT CUSTOM AUDIO DIRECTORY
==================================

You can drop your own custom audio files (.mp3, .wav, or .ogg) directly into this folder!
ElephantFit will automatically detect and play your custom audio files.

Supported Audio Filenames:
----------------------------------
1. trumpet.mp3   (or trumpet.wav)
   -> Played when Jumbo completes a workout, rears up in celebration, or when you click "Trumpet Call".

2. rumble.mp3    (or rumble.wav)
   -> Played when you click "Hear Jumbo", feed Jumbo, or during resting/recovery.

3. footstep.mp3  (or footstep.wav)
   -> Played when Jumbo takes steps or walks on the savanna trail.

4. chime.mp3     (or chime.wav)
   -> Played when switching camera view presets, completing reps, or updating biometrics.

5. warning.mp3   (or warning.wav)
   -> Played when Jumbo eats too many peanuts and triggers the Peanut Overload warning.

Note:
If any of these custom files are not present in this folder, ElephantFit automatically falls back to the built-in acoustic Web Audio synthesizer.
